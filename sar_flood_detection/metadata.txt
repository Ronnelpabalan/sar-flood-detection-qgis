description=Flood mapping from Sentinel-1 SAR, starting from the raw GRD product: terrain correction, speckle filtering, thresholding and change detection.
about=Processing tools that map surface water and floods from Sentinel-1. "Prepare Sentinel-1 GRD" reads the downloaded .SAFE.zip directly and produces terrain-corrected sigma0 without other software: calibration, thermal noise removal, precise orbits from ESA, Copernicus GLO-30 DEM plus EGM2008 geoid, Range-Doppler terrain correction and a layover/shadow mask (orbit files and DEM tiles are downloaded once and cached). The flood tools work on any Sentinel-1 backscatter raster: optional Lee or median speckle filtering, fixed or Otsu thresholds, and pre/post-event change detection, giving a class raster styled with a transparent background.
